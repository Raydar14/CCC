What is cannabis coaching?

A guide for using cannabis on purpose — not on autopilot.

Cannabis coaching is a collaborative, educational service. We don't prescribe, diagnose, or sell product. Instead, we help you get clear on why you're using cannabis, what you're actually putting in your body, and how to do it in a way that supports your goals — better sleep, less stress, creative focus, or simply a healthier relationship with the plant.

Think of it like a wellness coach who happens to deeply understand cannabinoids, terpenes, dosing, tolerance, and harm reduction. You bring the goals; we bring the map, the science, and an honest, stigma-free sounding board.

  • Clarify your goals and what "better" looks like for you
  • Decode labels, formats, and dispensary jargon
  • Build a "start low, go slow" plan that fits your life
  • Practice mindful use, tolerance resets, and intentional breaks
1:1 Personalized, confidential sessions built around your goals
0 Judgment, sales pressure, or one-size-fits-all advice
